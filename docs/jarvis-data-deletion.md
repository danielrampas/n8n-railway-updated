# JARVIS Data Deletion Instructions

**Last updated:** 14 October 2026

JARVIS is a personal AI assistant and automation service operated for private use.

## How to request deletion

To request deletion of data associated with JARVIS, contact the operator using the contact information listed in the JARVIS Privacy Policy and state that the request concerns deletion of JARVIS data.

Please identify the WhatsApp account, email address, or other identifier relevant to the data you want deleted. Do not include passwords, access tokens, recovery codes, or other secrets.

## Data covered by a deletion request

Where applicable, a valid deletion request may cover:

- stored conversation content;
- uploaded documents or media;
- automation records containing personal content;
- long-term memory records deliberately stored by JARVIS;
- other user-linked data held in systems controlled by the operator.

## Technical and legal limitations

Some information may remain temporarily in encrypted backups, infrastructure logs, or third-party systems where immediate deletion is technically impossible. Such data will expire or be removed according to the applicable retention process and will not be used for new purposes.

Data that must be retained to comply with applicable law, prevent abuse, maintain security, or resolve an active dispute may be retained only for the period necessary for that purpose.

## Processing time

Deletion requests will be handled as soon as reasonably practicable after the relevant account or data can be identified.

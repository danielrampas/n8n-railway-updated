## 2. Data processed

Depending on how JARVIS is used, the service may process information such as:

- WhatsApp message content and related message metadata;
- documents, images, audio files, or other files intentionally submitted to JARVIS;
- information required to perform requested automations;
- technical information needed for operation, security, troubleshooting, and execution logging.

JARVIS only processes information that is intentionally submitted to the service or is required to carry out an action requested by the authorized user.

## 3. Purpose of processing

Data is processed solely to provide the requested personal-assistant and automation functions, including answering messages, processing submitted content, executing authorized workflows, maintaining relevant conversational context, and operating and securing the service.

Data is not sold and is not used for third-party advertising.

## 4. Service providers

To operate JARVIS, data may be transmitted to service providers that are technically necessary to deliver the requested functionality. These may include Meta/WhatsApp, hosting and automation infrastructure providers, cloud storage or database providers, and AI model providers.

Only the data necessary for the relevant operation is intended to be shared with such providers, subject to their respective terms and privacy policies.

## 5. Data retention

Data is retained only for as long as needed for the purposes described above, for operation of the service, or where retention is technically necessary for backups, security, troubleshooting, or workflow history.

Where possible, temporary processing data is not retained longer than necessary. Long-term storage is used only where required by the requested functionality or deliberately configured by the operator.

## 6. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to protect JARVIS and its data, including access controls, authentication, encrypted network connections, restricted credentials, and controlled access to automation infrastructure.

No internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security.
